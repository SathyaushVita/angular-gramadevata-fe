Our Village - Our Temple - Our Deity

Gramadevata Foundation is a pioneering organization dedicated to the revival, preservation, and promotion of Sanatana Dharma and Hindu cultural heritage. Rooted in the ancient Indian principle of "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam" (The World is One Family), the foundation's core philosophy revolves around creating a unified global Hindu community that is deeply connected to its spiritual roots. The foundation’s efforts span across multiple domains, including temple preservation, community engagement, cultural education, and rural upliftment, all designed to foster a strong, vibrant, interconnected, and empowered Hindu society.

About Us – Grammadevata Foundation

Grammadevata Foundation - "Connect, Communicate, and Coordinate" with your roots.

At the heart of India's cultural and spiritual identity lie its temples, traditions, and timeless values. Grammadevata Foundation is a visionary initiative that bridges the ancient and the modern, connecting the global Hindu community and the global Sanatana community through the revival and preservation of our sacred heritage.

Rooted in the spirit of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam — “The World is One Family” — the foundation brings together a wide spectrum of cultural, religious, and educational initiatives. Our focus spans from creating and maintaining temple digital transformation web and app application for ancient iconic temples, famous temples, area temples, and village temples to documenting temple history, rituals, events and community stories.

We honor the legacy of Vishnu, Shiva, Devi temples and the sanctity of temple towns and large temples across India. Whether its town temples tucked in urban spaces or remote Sanatana temples in the heart of, we believe every temple has a story worth sharing.

Vision

The vision of Gramadevata Foundation is to "Connect, Communicate, and Coordinate" with the spiritual and cultural roots of Sanatana Dharma. The foundation aims to cultivate a sense of family and unity among Hindus worldwide by bridging the gap between modern life and ancient traditions. By leveraging technology and community-driven initiatives, the foundation envisions a world where every Hindu remains connected to their heritage, actively participates in spiritual and cultural activities, and contributes to the global Hindu community.

Mission

  • Gramadevata Foundation’s mission is multifaceted, focusing on empowering Hindutva through various platforms that cater to different aspects of Hindu life.

  • Protecting and revitalizing sacred temples across India, ensuring they remain active centers of spiritual practice and community engagement

  • Encouraging the practice of traditional rituals and customs in villages and urban areas alike, helping to sustain these practices for future generations.

  • Building a global network of Sanatana Dharma organizations to collaborate on cultural, trade, and political initiatives, thus amplifying the impact of Hindu values on the world stage.

  • Providing access to knowledge and resources on Hinduism, including scriptures, rituals, cultural arts, and traditional crafts, to educate and empower individuals.

  • Supporting rural artisans and small-scale producers by providing them with platforms to sell their products, thereby preserving traditional crafts and improving livelihoods.
